# Design context

- **Product**: Personal portfolio for Iqra Aslam, full-stack engineer in Faisalabad.
- **Audience**: Freelance clients and hiring engineers who judge the site in seconds.
- **Job**: Look finished, quiet, and expensive. Show live product screens. Make contact effortless.
- **Tone**: Sleek, minimal, engineering-driven. Deep zinc canvas, Geist, violet as a restrained accent, emerald for availability.
- **Not this**: Loud gradients, generic bento dashboards, cyan glow, cluttered cards, template SaaS hero.
